# Brand Profile: McDonald's

Provenance tags: **[Sourced]** = from a public source listed at the bottom.
**[Third-party]** = reported by a non-McDonald's source, treat as likely but unconfirmed.
**[Assumed]** = general brand positioning or our own judgment, edit freely.
McDonald's has no public creator brief or brand center, so verify against official
assets before anything is published.

## Voice and audience
- **Voice:** Warm, playful, inclusive, upbeat. Short, snackable lines. Light humor, never sarcastic or edgy. [Assumed]
- **Audience:** Broad: families, students, young adults 18-34, busy commuters, value-seekers and treat-seekers. [Assumed]
- **Core themes:** Togetherness, everyday moments, craveable food, value and convenience, "I'm lovin' it". [Assumed]

## Creator and fan-driven approach ("share the pen")
- Let fans and creators speak in their own voice. Content should feel like a real fan moment, not a polished ad. [Sourced: NRN]
- Build on what fans are already doing and saying (e.g. fans creating content around Famous Orders) rather than steering the conversation. [Sourced: NRN]
- Two rules govern creator work: brand safety and creator safety. [Sourced: NRN]
- Creator content works best as ongoing waves of posts, not a single burst. [Third-party: DesignRush]
- Creator content that performs organically can be boosted with paid media. [Third-party: Hashtag Paid]

## Visual authenticity (for image generation)
- **Look and feel:** Creator-style, in-the-moment, real-life settings: a car, a bedroom, a table with friends. Natural light, slightly imperfect framing. Avoid studio-ad gloss. [Sourced in spirit: NRN "share the pen"; specific style is Assumed]
- **Subject:** Lead with the food and the moment of enjoying it. Real-looking people, relaxed and unposed. [Assumed]
- **Colors:** Red and yellow are the brand palette. Starting values #DA291C (red) and #FFC72C (yellow); sources disagree on exact values, so confirm against official files. [Third-party]
- **Logo and arches:** Never rotate, stretch, recolor, or crop the logo, and keep clear space around it. [Third-party: brandkit.pro]
  Image models often garble logos and packaging text. Prefer compositions where the logo is small or absent and have a human add the official asset in post. [Assumed]
- **Products:** Show only real menu items. Do not invent menu items, packaging, or limited-time offers. [Assumed]

## Content rules
- **Do:** Short captions with a clear call to action (app, order, visit). Emoji sparingly. [Assumed]
- **Don't:** Make health or nutrition claims. Compare with competitors. Invent prices or promotions. [Assumed]
- **Hashtags:** Brand-led and short, e.g. #ImLovinIt. [Assumed]
- **Sponsored or creator-partnered content:** Must carry a clear disclosure of the brand relationship (FTC "material connection" rule). Confirm exact wording against the FTC's own endorsement guidance. [Third-party: EPR guide]

## Reusing fan content
- Reuse of a fan's content requires their permission. In Ireland McDonald's collects this via a hashtag reply (#McDonaldsUGCyes), and participants must be 18 or over. Treat this as a pattern, as terms vary by country. [Sourced: McDonald's Ireland UGC terms]
- Never generate content that depicts or impersonates a real, identifiable person or creator. [Assumed]
