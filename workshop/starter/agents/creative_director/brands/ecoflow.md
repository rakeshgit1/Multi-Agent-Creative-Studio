## Voice and audience
- **Voice:** Motivational, clean, science-backed. [Provided]
  Encouraging coach, not drill sergeant: short, confident lines, plain language, light on jargon. Positive framing ("keep your flow going"), never guilt or shame about missed water goals. [Assumed]
- **Audience:** Health-conscious millennials, 25-35. [Provided]
  Includes gym-goers, runners and hikers, desk workers building healthier habits, and sustainability-minded buyers who like useful tech. [Assumed]
- **Core themes:** Hydration tracking, 24-hour cold retention, sustainability. [Provided]
  Supporting themes: small daily habits, active lifestyle, "less single-use plastic", feeling good and energised. [Assumed]
- **Tagline direction:** Short and rhythmic, built on flow, habit and refill (e.g. "Stay in your flow"). Treat as a starting idea, not an approved tagline. [Assumed]

## Product truths (use only these as claims)
- Smart hydration tracking. [Provided]
- Keeps drinks cold for 24 hours. [Provided]
- Reusable bottle positioned as a sustainable alternative to single-use plastic. [Provided, framing Assumed]
- Do not invent other specs (battery life, app features, capacity, materials, certifications, recycled-content percentages) unless supplied in the brief. [Assumed]

## Creator and community approach
- Let real users show their routines: morning run, gym bag, desk, trail. Content should feel like a genuine day-in-the-life moment, not a polished ad. [Assumed]
- Celebrate streaks and small wins from the community rather than pushing perfection. [Assumed]
- Fitness, wellness and outdoors creators are a natural fit; two rules govern creator work: brand safety and creator safety. [Assumed]
- Creator content works best as ongoing waves tied to habits (e.g. a weekly hydration check-in) rather than a single burst. [Assumed]
- High-performing organic posts can be boosted with paid media. [Assumed]

## Visual style (for image generation)
- **Look and feel:** Clean, bright, minimal, active-lifestyle imagery. [Provided]
  Natural daylight, airy negative space, crisp focus on the bottle, candid movement over rigid poses. [Assumed]
- **Settings:** Morning trail, gym, yoga studio, bike path, tidy desk, kitchen counter with a refill. [Assumed]
- **Subject:** Lead with the bottle in use (sipping, refilling, checking progress) within a real moment. Real-looking, diverse people, relaxed and active, not stock-model stiff. [Assumed]
- **Colors:** Fresh, water-inspired palette: aqua/teal and clean white with soft green accents for the sustainability angle. No official hex values exist; confirm before use. Suggested starting points: #00A6A6 (teal), #FFFFFF (white), #7BC47F (soft green). [Assumed]
- **Composition:** Minimal backgrounds, plenty of whitespace, one clear focal point, condensation or water-droplet details to signal cold retention. [Assumed]
- **Logo and text:** Image models often garble logos, bottle labels and on-screen app text. Prefer compositions where the logo is small, plain or absent and have a human add the official asset in post. Do not render fake app screens with readable numbers. [Assumed]
- **Products:** Show a single, simple bottle design consistently. Do not invent variants, accessories, packaging or limited editions. [Assumed]
- **Avoid:** Cluttered scenes, heavy filters, neon or gritty gym looks, single-use plastic bottles shown positively, studio gloss that feels unrealistic. [Assumed]

## Content rules
- **Do:** Short captions with a clear call to action (shop, learn more, join the challenge). Emoji sparingly, water and nature ones fit best. Lead with a benefit, then the proof point. [Assumed]
- **Don't:**
  - Make medical or health-outcome claims (e.g. weight loss, curing fatigue, "detox"). Frame hydration as a habit, not a treatment. [Assumed]
  - Make unverified sustainability claims (e.g. "carbon neutral", "100% recyclable", "saves X bottles") without data. Keep green messaging specific and honest to avoid greenwashing. [Assumed]
  - Compare with or name competitors. [Assumed]
  - Invent prices, discounts, promotions or giveaways. [Assumed]
  - Shame users for not hitting hydration goals. [Assumed]
- **Hashtags:** Short and brand-led, e.g. #EcoFlow, #StayInYourFlow, #RefillNotLandfill. [Assumed]
- **Sponsored or creator-partnered content:** Must carry a clear disclosure of the brand relationship (FTC "material connection" rule). Confirm exact wording against the FTC's own endorsement guidance. [Assumed]

## Platform notes
- **Instagram / TikTok:** Vertical, movement-led, quick routine clips and refill moments; first second shows the bottle. [Assumed]
- **LinkedIn / workplace angle:** Desk-hydration and healthy-habit framing, calmer and more informational. [Assumed]
- **X / short text:** One punchy line plus one proof point (tracking or 24-hour cold). [Assumed]

## Reusing fan content
- Reuse of a fan's content requires their explicit permission, and participants should be 18 or over. [Assumed]
- Never generate content that depicts or impersonates a real, identifiable person or creator. [Assumed]
